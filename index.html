Theory of Double and Triple Integration

Double Integration

A double integral accumulates values of a function of two variables over a 2D region R:

∬R f(x, y) dA

For a rectangular region x ∈ [a, b], y ∈ [c, d], it can be evaluated as:

∫cd ∫ab f(x, y) dx dy

Triple Integration

A triple integral accumulates values of a function of three variables over a 3D region V:

∭V f(x, y, z) dV

In a box-shaped region x ∈ [a, b], y ∈ [c, d], z ∈ [e, f], we write:

∫ef ∫cd ∫ab f(x, y, z) dx dy dz

Applications

  • Area and volume computation
  • Mass and center of mass with density functions
  • Heat and probability distributions in multiple dimensions
